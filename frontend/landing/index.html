A Letter from the Founder

Every part has a second road ahead.

In 1998 I started this shop in Cebu City with nothing but a few tools and a promise to be honest about every part I sold.

Today our stock comes from Japanese and South Korean half-cuts and carefully chosen local salvage. Each part is dismantled by hand, cleaned, and checked before it goes on the shelf.

This business raised my family. Every customer who drives away with the right part is the reason we're still here.

— Roel Tangpos Batawang, Founder
