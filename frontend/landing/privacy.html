Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Who we are

Rovi Autoparts Surplus is a sole proprietorship founded in 1998 by Roel Tangpos Batawang, selling surplus auto parts from Panganiban Street, Cebu City, Cebu 6000, Philippines. We run this website and the shop app, and we are responsible for the personal information you give us through them.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address, password (stored only as a secure one-way hash), and, if you choose to add them, your mobile number, address and notes about your vehicles.
  • Reservations: the parts you reserve, quantities, your preferred pickup date, any notes you leave for the shop and, once you collect, how you paid and the receipt or transaction number.
  • Chat messages: the messages you exchange with our team, including any part you attach to a message.
  • Profile photo: if you upload one.
  • Your location, only when you use "How far am I?" on the Visit page. Your browser asks for permission first. The location is used inside your browser to draw the route and estimate the distance; it is never sent to or stored by us.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To hold the parts you reserve, prepare them for pickup and record the sale when you collect.
  • To answer your questions about fitment, stock and your reservations by chat.
  • To protect the shop and our customers from fraud and abuse, for example by limiting repeated failed sign-ins.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies

We use only two cookies, both strictly necessary: a session cookie (RA_SESSION) that keeps you signed in, and a security cookie (XSRF-TOKEN) that protects your account against cross-site request forgery. We do not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies.

Services we rely on

  • Website and app hosting providers, which serve the pages and run the shop's server.
  • A database provider, which stores accounts, reservations and messages.
  • An image storage provider, which stores part photos and profile photos.
  • OpenStreetMap, whose map tiles are loaded by your browser on the Visit page.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account for as long as it is open. Reservation records are kept as part of the shop's sales history. Chat messages are kept so we can refer back to past questions about your vehicle. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below).

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, object to its processing, or have it deleted. You can update most of your details yourself in My profile.

How to delete your data

Email us at vinceran2003@gmail.com or send us a message in the shop chat from your account, and tell us you'd like your account and data deleted. We'll confirm the request with you and delete your account, profile photo and chat history. Records of completed sales may be kept where the law requires it.

How we protect your information

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies that scripts can't read, with protection against cross-site request forgery. Accounts are temporarily locked after repeated failed sign-ins, and uploaded images are checked before they are stored.

Children

The shop is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the shop chat.
