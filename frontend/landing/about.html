Heritage

Started fromscratch.

Roel Tangpos Batawang opened Rovi Autoparts Surplus on Panganiban Street in 1998 with a handful of tools, a borrowed corner of floor space and no stock to speak of.

He built the trade one part at a time — buying what he could afford, cleaning it by hand and selling it with an honest word about its condition. Mechanics came back, then their customers did, and the yard slowly grew.

The shop became the income that raised his family: Roel and his wife brought up their two children on it. The same promise still stands behind the counter today — the right part, described honestly, at a fair surplus price.
