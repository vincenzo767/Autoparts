Reservations, payment and pickup

  • No online payment. Reserving a part holds it for you; you pay at the counter when you collect.
  • Holds. A reserved part is held for 7 days, or until two days after your preferred pickup date if that is later. We can extend a hold if you message us.
  • Automatic release. If a reservation isn't collected before its hold ends, it is cancelled automatically and the parts go back on sale. We'll let you know in the chat.
  • Payment at the counter. We accept cash, GCash, Maya, card and bank transfer.
  • Please bring your reservation reference (for example RA-260927-K7QM) when you collect.
  • You can cancel a reservation online while it is awaiting confirmation or confirmed. Once it is ready for pickup, please message us.

Prices and availability

Prices are in Philippine pesos and may change. The price you pay is the price shown when you reserved. Surplus stock is often one of a kind; a part is only held once your reservation is placed.

Condition, fitment and returns

Every part is inspected and given a condition grade. Grade A parts are clean and fully working, tested where possible; Grade B parts work with normal wear or cosmetic marks. Tire tread is shown as a percentage of new.

Fitment information is a guide. Please send us your vehicle's make, model and year before you reserve, and check the part at pickup. Ask us about our check-warranty and return terms before you buy — they are confirmed in person at the counter.
